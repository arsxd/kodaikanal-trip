Driving on weekends

  • Ghat roads are single-lane in stretches; tourist traffic adds 15–30 min to inter-cluster drives on weekends
  • Start before 8 AM to clear the town-to-Poombarai stretch while roads are empty
  • Poombarai → Mannavannur road passes through shola forest; expect fog before 9 AM
  • Fill up in Kodaikanal town — no fuel stations on the Poombarai or Mannavannur routes
  • Mobile signal drops between Poombarai and Mannavannur; download offline maps
  • Mannavannur area may need a forest department permit — check at the Kodai forest office before heading out
