Route Alerts & Driver Checklist

  • Fuel: Full tank at Palani ghat foot. Zero petrol pumps on the entire western ridge (Poombarai / Kookal / Mannavanur / Poondi).
  • Offline Maps: Mobile signal drops past Poombarai. Pre-download the Google Maps offline area for Kodaikanal.
  • Dolphin's Nose Timing: The 05:45 start is crucial — parking fits <10 cars and the trail beats morning heat.
  • Descent: Use 2nd/3rd gear engine braking on the 64 hairpins down to Palani.
  • Buffer: Leaving Kodai town at 20:00 gives 5h 45m of slack before a 01:45 arrival at Palakkad Station.
